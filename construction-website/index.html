How it works

Five stages. No surprises.

Fixed-price contracts, a dedicated project manager and a live schedule you can check any time.

  1. 01

    Consult & budget

    We walk the site, learn your brief and give you an honest range before any drawings are paid for.

  2. 02

    Design & permits

    We coordinate architects, engineers and the council so approvals land on schedule.

  3. 03

    Site preparation

    Survey, clearing, grading and foundations — the groundwork for everything that follows.

  4. 04

    Construction

    Framing, envelope, services and finishes, with weekly photo updates and milestone inspections.

  5. 05

    Handover & care

    A detailed walkthrough, a documented home manual and a 10-year structural warranty.
